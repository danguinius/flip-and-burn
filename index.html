About the data

Unofficial fan project. Any resemblance to a certain series of space novels is entirely affectionate. Not affiliated with any author, publisher or studio.

Planets use JPL's approximate Keplerian elements (Standish), fitted for 1800–2050; accuracy degrades outside that span, so the 2350s are a plausible sky, not an ephemeris. Ceres, Vesta, Pallas, Hygiea and Eros use JPL osculating elements propagated two-body, without perturbations. Moons use real radii and periods; only Luna's phase is approximately real. The made-up stations have invented orbits.

Route math treats ships as straight-line torch flights with no solar gravity, which is fair at torch-drive thrust and poor at low thrust. Torch routes match the target's velocity on arrival. Hohmann figures are patched-conic heliocentric Δv only, without the departure climb or capture burn.
